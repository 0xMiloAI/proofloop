Designed for trust from both sides.

The public profile is shareable, but the collection process keeps reviewers protected.

Are reviews anonymous?

Public profiles show aggregated themes and approved excerpts. Reviewer identity can stay private.

Can weak signals be hidden?

The profile should be credible, so it shows confidence, coverage, and recency instead of only praise.

Is this a resume replacement?

No. It works beside a resume or portfolio by adding evidence from people who saw the work happen.

Who controls the link?

The candidate owns sharing. Teams can request access, but candidates choose what they publish.
